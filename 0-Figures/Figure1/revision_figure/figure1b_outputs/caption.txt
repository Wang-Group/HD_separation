Figure 1B. Original H/D separation coefficients versus reviewed LSV overpotential. Horizontal bars show mean +/- sample SD of accepted eta10 measurements (n >= 2). Diamonds denote mean rising/nearest-point estimates for samples without accepted eta10 and are not measured eta10; their bars, where present, are estimate SDs. Scan-only samples are shown at their furthest tested limit, not as averaged eta10. Open left triangles at 10 mV represent samples with no usable result whose recorded current magnitude remains above 10 mA/cm2; this is a display placeholder, not measured eta10 or evidence of high HER activity. These samples are excluded from the Pareto front. Means and uncertainty endpoints outside 10-550 mV are projected to the display boundary only. Blue and red lines show literature and combined nominal Pareto fronts; rising points and bounds are excluded. Audited same-recipe measurements are pooled once under ordinary catalyst names. No new HD repeats are used.
